# 11. Sales COGS FIFO Valuation, Customer Debt, and Finance Settlement

Sales invoices record goods dispatched to customers from a designated warehouse, establishing accounts receivable (`debtBalance`) and double-entry accounting lines (Debit 4010, Credit 9010, Credit 6410). Inventory batches are consumed strictly in FIFO sequence to derive actual landed cost COGS (Debit 9110, Credit 2910) and economic gross profit (Net Revenue excluding VAT minus COGS). Operational cash receipts are recorded strictly within the Finance module as income transactions and allocated either to a specific sales invoice or distributed across open customer invoices via FIFO. A posted sales invoice cannot be unposted or cancelled while linked finance payments or sales returns exist (Sales Rollback Invariant).
