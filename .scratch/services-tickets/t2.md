## What to build

Posting engine, counterparty debt accrual, and dual-entry journal integration for Service Acts:

- `DRAFT` → `POSTED` transition that accrues counterparty debt.
- `PROVIDED`: increments Customer Debt (`Debitorlik`) on the counterparty, creates Debit 4010 / Credit 9030 (revenue) / Credit 6410 (output VAT) journal entries.
- `RECEIVED`: increments Supplier Debt (`Kreditorlik`) on the counterparty, creates Debit 9420/9430 (expense) / Debit 4410 (input VAT) / Credit 6010 (payables) journal entries.
- Immutability of posted documents — header and line items cannot be modified once `status === POSTED`.
- Safe cancellation (`cancel`) and unpost-to-draft (`unpost`) for acts with zero linked payments, reversing debt and storno-ing journal entries.
- Service Act details modal (`ServiceActDetailsModal`) showing posted status, debt delta, and ledger entries.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Posting a `PROVIDED` act increments the counterparty's `customerDebt` and `debtBalance` by `totalAmount`.
- [ ] Posting a `RECEIVED` act increments the counterparty's `supplierDebt` and decrements `debtBalance` by `totalAmount`.
- [ ] Journal entries Debit 4010 / Credit 9030 / Credit 6410 are created atomically on `PROVIDED` post.
- [ ] Journal entries Debit 9420/9430 / Debit 4410 / Credit 6010 are created atomically on `RECEIVED` post.
- [ ] Attempting to modify line items or header of a `POSTED` act returns HTTP 400.
- [ ] Cancelling an unpaid posted act reverses counterparty debt and deletes associated journal entries.
- [ ] Unposting an unpaid posted act reverts it to `DRAFT`, reversing debt and journal entries.
