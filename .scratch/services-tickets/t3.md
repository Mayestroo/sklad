## What to build

Decoupled Finance module settlement, the Service Rollback Invariant guardrail, and the printable dalolatnoma:

- Contextual "Moliya to'lovi kiritish" (Record Finance Payment) button on the service act details view that navigates to the Finance module with counterparty and act ID pre-filled — **no cash controls inside the Services module itself**.
- Finance module extended to accept `sourceDocType: 'ServiceAct'` and `sourceDocId` on `INCOME` and `EXPENSE` transactions, atomically incrementing `ServiceAct.paidAmount` and recalibrating `paymentStatus` (`UNPAID` → `PARTIALLY_PAID` → `PAID`) with each recorded payment.
- **Service Rollback Invariant**: attempting to cancel or delete a posted act when `paidAmount > 0` or linked `FinanceTransaction` records exist returns HTTP 400.
- Printable / PDF-exportable official "Xizmatlar ko'rsatish dalolatnomasi" (Certificate of Completed Work) layout compliant with Uzbekistan commercial document norms, including company and counterparty rekvizitlar, itemized table, totals, VAT line, and signature / stamp blocks.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Recording an `INCOME` finance transaction linked to a `PROVIDED` service act increments `paidAmount` by the payment amount and transitions `paymentStatus` to `PARTIALLY_PAID` or `PAID` as appropriate.
- [ ] Recording an `EXPENSE` finance transaction linked to a `RECEIVED` service act settles the corresponding supplier debt.
- [ ] Attempting to cancel or delete a posted act that has `paidAmount > 0` returns HTTP 400 with a descriptive Uzbek error message.
- [ ] Attempting to cancel or delete a posted act with linked `FinanceTransaction` records returns HTTP 400 even if `paidAmount` is zero.
- [ ] The printable act view renders correct company info, counterparty info, itemized service lines, subtotal, VAT, total, and signature blocks.
- [ ] No cash or bank mutation button exists anywhere in the Services module UI.
