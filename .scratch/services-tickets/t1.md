## What to build

Core document lifecycle, auto-calculations, and draft management interface for Service Acts:

- Full CRUD operations with deterministic auto-generated document numbering (`ACT-YYYY-XXXX`).
- Unified nomenclature catalog integration: auto-populate default rates, measurement units, and VAT rates (0% or 12%) when selecting services (`ProductType = SERVICE`), with support for custom line descriptions.
- Line subtotal, VAT amount, and grand total decimal arithmetic calculations (`lineTotal = (qty * unitPrice) + vatAmount`; `totalAmount = sum(lineTotal)`).
- Dedicated frontend views (`/services`) with segmented tabs for "Ko'rsatilgan xizmatlar" (`PROVIDED`) and "Olingan xizmatlar" (`RECEIVED`), and a slide-over creation/editing drawer containing strictly zero cash/bank handling elements.
- `DRAFT` acts can be edited and deleted without altering counterparty debt.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Operator can create a new draft service act (`PROVIDED` or `RECEIVED`) with multiple itemized lines.
- [ ] Selecting a service from the product catalog automatically populates default unit of measure, unit price, and VAT rate.
- [ ] Operator can enter custom service descriptions, adjust quantities, and modify unit prices.
- [ ] System automatically calculates `lineTotal = (quantity * unitPrice) + vatAmount` and document `totalAmount`.
- [ ] Draft acts can be updated or deleted without altering counterparty debt balances.
- [ ] The services interface contains zero cash or bank payment controls, maintaining strict separation of concerns.
